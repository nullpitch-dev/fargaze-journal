- 새로 배운 단어 / 표현
	- **attire**: clothes, especially of a particular or formal type
	- **being made redundant**: having lost your job because your employer no longer needs you
	- **blatant**: very obvious and intentional, when this is a bad thing (뻔뻔스러운)
	- **cicada**(/sɪˈkɑː.də/): 매미
	- **delirious**: extremely happy or excited
		- Also means: unable to think or speak clearly because of fever or mental confusion
	- **devastated** (/ˈdev.ə.steɪ.tɪd/): completely destroyed; very shocked and upset
	- **downer**: an event or experience that makes you unhappy
		- Financial concerns are the biggest downer for me.
		- You lost your job? That's a real downer.
	- **drama queen**: someone who gets too upset or angry over small problems
	- **fib**: a mall lie that does not cause any harm (noun and verb)
		- Apart from the little fibs, she did nothing wrong.
		- I can tell he's fibbing because he's smiling!
	- **fossilized error**: a grammatical, pronunciation or vocabulary mistake that has become a permanent habit
	- **get to grips with something**: to make an effort to understand and deal with a problem or situation
		- I'm still trying to get to grips with this new smartphone.
	- **good riddance**: said when you are pleased that a bad or unwanted thing or person, or something of poor quality, has gone (상대방에게 쓸 때는 위험한 표현)
	- **gust**: a sudden strong wind
	- **incessant** (/ɪnˈses.ənt/): never stopping, escpecially in an annoying or unpleasant way
	- **lethargic** (/ləˈθɑː.dʒɪk/): having little energy; feeling unwilling and unable to do anything
	- **looming**: (of something unwanted or unpleasant) about to happen soon and causing worry
		- the looming crisis
		- I have to submit this report. It is a looming deadline.
	- **out and about**: active; doing the things you usually do
		- The doctor says she's making a good recovery, and she should be out and about in a few
			days's time.
	- **patchwork**: 누더기
	- **precarious**: in a dangerous state because of not being safe or not being held in place firmly
		- The lorry was lodged in a very precarious way, with its fron wheels hanging over the
			cliff.
		- Her suitcase was precariously balanced on the tiny luggage rack above her head.
	- **put somebody on the spot**: put someone in a difficult or embarrassing situation
		- Mira rather put the boss on the spot by asking him when he was going to give us a pay
			rise.
	- **sanity**:
		- the fact of showing good judgement and understanding
			- The closer we got to the deadline for action, the more I questoined the **sanity of
				the decision** we had taken.
	- **scrape through** / **get through** / **sail through**
		- scrape(/skreɪp/) through: to only just succeed/finish with a lot of effort
		- get thgouth: to succeed/finish with some effort
			- ※ to succeed in talking to someone on the phone
		- sail through: to succeed/finish with ease
	- **see through**: to help or support someone during a difficult period in their life
		- My brother lent me ＄500 to see me through the next few weeks.
	- **slur**: to pronounce the sounds of a word in a way that is wrong or not clear
		- Her speech was slurred.
	- **snag**: a problem, difficulty, or disadvantage
		- The drug is very effective - the only snag is that it cannot be produced in large
			quantities.
	- **tacit**: understood without being expressed directly
	- **tedious**: boring and tiring, esp. because long or often repeated
	- **up for (doing) something**:
		- willing and able to do or take part in an activity
			- We're going swimming. Are you up for it?
		- intended, suggested, or being considered for something
			- Are you really up for promotion?
	- **verbatim** (/vɜːˈbeɪ.tɪm/, adverb): in a way that uses exactly the same words as were originally used
		- She had an amazing memory and could recall verbatim quite complex conversations.
	- **whinge**: to complain, especially about something that does not seem important
		- Stop whinging.
- #### 틀리게 / 부정확하게 알고 있었던 내용
	- **most people** vs. **most of the people**: in general vs. a specific group
	- **change**: 자동사로 쓰일 때 수동태 아님
		- The taxonomy can change at any time
	- **settle in**: 역시 능동태로 사용. We settled in smoothly.
	- **match**도 능동태로 사용. to be matched with something (x), to match something (o)
	- **at the end** vs. **in the end**: at the end는 문자 그대로 the end를 의미. 시간의 끝. 물체의 끝. in the end는 finally / ulimately / eventually의 의미.
	- **I'm worried** vs. **I'm worrying**: I'm worrying이 틀린 말은 아닌데 의미가 다르고 그런 표현을 거의 안함.
	- **similar to** vs. **similar with**: 'similar with'는 틀린 문법. compared with와 혼동하면 안됨.
	- **compared to** vs. **compared with**: to와 with를 섞어 쓰는데 to는 비유에 가깝고 with는 비교에 가까움
	- **celebrate**는 타동사. celebrate for xxx는 틀린 문법.
	- **agree**:
		- **with**: [Someone] I agree with you.
		- **to**: [An Action/Plan] I agree to update the script (action). Did the school agree to our request? (proposal)
		- **on**: [An Decision/Item] We agreed on the price of the golf clubs.
		- **about**: [A General Topic] We agree about politics.
	- I could speak more than when I **take** private classes. (일반적인 사실이나 평소의 경험과 비교할 때는 과거형이 아니라 현재형을 사용)
	- **Please be aware** or **Please note**: Please be noted 는 틀린 문법
	- 고유명사에 대한 정관사 다시 정리
		- Names of People and Places: NO
		- Organizatoins, Schools, and Building Names: YES
		- Oceans, Seas, and Rivers: YES
		- Plural Names: YES
		- | **Category** | **❌ No "the"** | **Yes "the"** |
		  | ---- | ---- | ---- |
		  | **Schools** | ACS Cobham | **The** ACS International School |
		  | **Countries** | South Korea, France | **The** UK, **The** USA |
		  | **Golf/Sports** | Silvermere Golf Club | **The** Esher driving range |
		  | **People** | Alexander, Hyoje | **The** Cho family *(Plural group)* |
	- **fever**, **cough**: 구체적인 증상을 나타내기 때문에 가산 명사. **a**를 붙여야 됨.
