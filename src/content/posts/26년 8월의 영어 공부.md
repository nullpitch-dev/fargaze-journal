- 강사의 권고
- 새로 배운 단어 / 표현
	- <a
		href="https://www.oxfordinternationalenglish.com/30-useful-english-idiomatic-expressions-their-meanings/"
		target="_blank">**Idioms**</a>
	- **anesthetic**(/ˌæn.əsˈθet.ɪk/): a substance that makes you unable to feel pain
	- **intimidating**: making you feel frightened or nervous
		- an intimidating manner / She can be very intimidating when she's angry.
	- **meteorology**: the scientific study of the processes that casue particular weather conditions
	- **overly**: too or very (Your views on economics are overly simplistic. / I'm not
		overly fond of cats.) ※ 부정적이거나 과할 때만 사용
	- **precipitation**: water that falls from clouds towards the ground, especially as rain or snow
	- **stern**: severe, or showing disapproval
	- **주유**: refuel / fill up / fill up the tank / fill up with petrol
	- **타이어 지렁이 메움**: plug the hole (아니면 repaire the puncture)
	- **꼬치**: skewer
- 틀리게 / 부정확하게 알고 있었던 내용
	- I drove **for** 2 hours. (전치사 for 필요. 운전 시간을 목적어로 쓰면 안됨.)
	- because of **the** cloudy weather (특정한 날씨를 지칭할 때는 정관사 필요)
	- check-in / check-out을 동사로 쓸 때는 하이픈을 안 써야됨. After checking out ...
	- **consider** 다음에는 동명사가 옴 (부정사 X)
	- **help** somebody **with doing** something 은 잘 안쓴다.
		- help somebody do something 또는 help somebody with something 으로 표현
		- She helped me with washing the dishes (X)
		- She helped me wash the dishes (O) / She helped me with the dishes (O)
	- **interest**:
		- Uncountable: The feeling itself. (She has a lot of interest in golf.)
		- Countable: One specific topic/activity. (Golf is an interest of mine.)
	- math: 영국 영어에서는 복수로 **maths**라고 씀.
	- 요일 앞에 next/this/last 등이 오면 **on**을 생략함
		- on next Monday (X), next Monday (O)
	- **since** 다음에 duration을 쓰지 않도록 주의. (fixed starting point가 와야 됨)
		- since the day before yesterday (O) since two days ago (X)
		- duration을 쓸거면 for를 사용. (for the last two days)
	- start **on** xx and end **on** xx:
		- 며칠 "부터"라고 해서 start **from**으로 쓰지 않고 항상 **on**
		- 어떤 위치 이후로 시작된 경우의 어디서 "부터"도 **from**을 쓰면 안되고 **on**
			- It rained **from** the 17th hole. (X)  It started raining **on** the 17th hole.
	- **squats**, **push-ups**, **pull-ups** 등 반복 운동은 복수형으로 씀
	- **teeth**: 때우는 건 **on**, 통증은 **in**
	- **thankful** / **grateful**의 주어는 사람이어야 함 (**It**이 아님)
		- I was so thankful / grateful
	- **this time**: "이번에는"이라고 할 때 for this time 이 아니고 그냥 this time
	- tire **on**: one tire **on** my car was flat (**of** 아님)
		- **on**: if it touches the car physically (tires, doors, windshield, license plate)
		- **of**: if it is a property or specification (color, price, size, model)
	- **travel** vs. **trip**: travel은 일반적인 '여행 행위/이동'. trip은 일정 기간을 가진 구체적인 '여행'
	- 걸어갔다: **walked to** somewhere / visited somewhere **on foot** (by walk 은 틀린 표현)
	- (나를 포함한)우리 셋: **the three of us**
- 입에 붙게 연습할 내용
	- **leave**: left most of the dinner / leave the window open / leave someone alone / left the children playing
	- **different from how xx**:
		- The city feels very different from how it felt ten years ago.
		- Living in the UK is different from how I imagined it would be.
		- The movie was quite different from how I thought it would end.
		- The actual golf course was different from how it looked on TV.
	- **What should I** xx **when I mean** xx **rather than** xx?:
	- I don't think it **was worth** ￡xx for xx
	- It will be / was xx **days long**
	- **conference about**: conference의 목적을 관계 대명사로 구구절절 쓰지 말고 about 하나로.
	- it turned out **not to be completely resolved**
