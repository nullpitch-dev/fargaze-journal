- 새로 배운 단어 / 표현
	- **anecdote**: a short, often funny story, especially about something someone has done
	- **conscientious**(/ˌkɒn.ʃiˈen.ʃəs/): putting a lot of effort into your work. serious about your work and putting a lot of effort into doing it in a careful way.
		- a conscientious student / a conscientious worker
	- **cutback**: a reduction in something, made in order to save money
		- The closure of the printing factory is the company's biggest single cutback so far.
		- The company has announced cutbacks in production.
	- **fairly**: more than average, but less than very / more than a little; to some degree
		- She's farily tall. / I'm fairly sure that this is the right address. / I saw her fairly recently. / I know him fairly well.
	- **feel up to** something: to have the energy to do something
		- I don't feel up to going out tonight.
	- **finish strong**: 유종의 미
	- **makeshift**: temporary and of low quality, but used because of a sudden need
		- Thousands of refugees are living in makeshift camps.
	- **paddling pool**: a small toy pool for small children to play in
	- **procrastinate**(/prəˈkræs.tɪ.neɪt/): to keep delaying something that must be done, often because it is unpleasant or boring
		- I had been meaning to do it, but I kept procrastinating.
	- **roe**: fish eggs, eaten as food
	- **starting is half the battle**: 시작이 반이다.
	- **verdict**(/ˈvɜː.dɪkt/): an opinion or decision made after judging the facts that are given, especially one made at the end of a trial
		- Voters gave their vertict on the government's economic record last night.
	- **wash the bedding**: better expression than "doing laundry for bed sheets"
- 틀리게 / 부정확하게 알고 있었던 내용
	- **articles**:
		- <a href="https://getintoenglish.com/the-phrases-definite-article/"
			target="_blank">Definite Article</a>
		- 스포츠나 차트에서 등수를 말할 때는 정관사를 안쓴다.
			- took 3rd place, came in 1st place, finished in 2nd place
		- **soup** vs. **a soup**: 원칙적으로 불가산 명사가 맞으나 특정한 한 종류의 요리나 식당에서 나오는 한 그릇을 의미할 때는 가산 명사로 취급. 그리고 형용사의 수식을 받을 때도 "a"를 붙임.
			- I like soup. (일반적인 'Soup')
			- This is **a** delicious Korean soup. (특정한 '한 종류'의 탕/국 요리)
			- Can I get **a** soup and **a** salad? (식당에서 주문하는 '한 그릇')
			- **a** normal stew / **a** thick soup
		- Lunch, dinner 등도 형용사의 수식(like late, quick, delicious, or heavy)을 받으면 부정관사를 쓴다. (it turns into a countable description of that specific meal event.)
			- "We had **a** late lunch." (describes a specific kind of lunch)
			- "We had **a** quick breakfast before leaving."
			- "She cooked **a** delicious dinner for us."
		- 고유명사라도 유명한 건축물이나 공연장 이름 앞에는 보통 the가 붙고, 반대로 기차역/전철역 이름 앞에는 the를 쓰지 않는다.
	- had chatting (X), **had a chat** (O), **chatted** (O)
	- **deserve**: 목적어를 바로 쓰던지 infinitive를 사용해야 함. deserve -ing (X) deserve to verb (O) deserve somthing (O)
	- **familiar with** vs. **familiar to**
		- 사람이 무언가에 익숙할 때: I am familiar **with** this course.
		- 사물/개념이 주어일 때: This course is familiar **to** me.
	- **gerund** vs. **infinitive**
		- Future, goals, intentions, or hypothetical actions: **Infinitives**
			- I hope to visit Lodon. (Future goal)
			- He decided to buy a car. (Intention before the action)
			- She wants to learn English. (Desire for the future)
		- Past actions, ongoing real experiences, or completed events: **Gerunds**
			- I enjoy playing golf. (An ongoing real experience you do)
			- He admitted making a mistake. (Refers to a past completed action)
			- She finished cooking dinner. (Completed action)
		- Activities in general or abstract concept: **Gerunds**
			- I love cooking. (The general activity/hobby)
			- Swimming is good for your health. (Subject of a sentence)
		- A specific instance or choice: **Infinitives**
			- I like to cook dinner early on Sundays. (A specific habit/choice)
		- Can take both, but the meaning shifts:
			- I remember meeting him. vs. Please remember to meet him.
			- I stopped smoking. vs. I stopped to skome.
			- Try adding more salt (Experiment & Solution). vs. I tried to lift the heavy box (Effort & Struggle)
	- a good **night's** sleep: good sleep 이라고 안함.

	- **present perfect** vs. **simple past**:
		- 과거 특정 시점: Simple past (I went to Madrid two years ago.)
		- 특정할 수 없는 과거. 시점이 중요하지 않은 과거의 경험: Present perfect (I've been to Spain.)
		- 과거 일인데 현재 시점에 중요하지 않은 경우: Simple past (Did you do your homework?)
		- 과거 일인데 현재와 연관이 있는 경우. 과거 이벤트로 인해 지금이 어떤 지가 중요한 경우: Present perfect (Have you done your homework?)
		- Just, already, yet 등도 현재와 relation이 있다고 봐야 됨: Present perfect (I haven't done it yet)
		- <a href="https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/present-perfect" target="_blank">Present Perfect</a>
		- (한 줄 요약) 지금 내 눈앞에 그 영향이 남아있는가?
	- **prevent A from B**
		- **prevent something to happen**은 틀렸고 **prevent something from happending**이 맞음
			- The rain prevented us from playing golf. (O)
			- The rain prevented us to play golf. (X)
			- This medicine will prevent you from getting sick. (O)
	- **redundant in xx** vs. **redundant with xx**
		- Redundant in xx means it's superfluous inside xx; redundant with xx means it duplicates xx
	- **reported speech**:
		- Fact 또는 일반적인 사실: Present (I asked the teacher and she said the sky is bule.)
		- 과거 시점에서 아직 안 일어난 사건: Present (오전 10시 시점에. I asked the teacher about the homework and she said it **needs** to be done by 5pm today. He said the party **starts** at 9pm tomorrow.)
		- <a
			href="https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/b1-b2/reported-speech-statements"
			target="_blank">Reported Speech</a>
	- sing along **to** the music: to를 붙여야 됨.
	- **suggested for someone to do something** (X) **suggested that somenone do something** (O)
		- I suggested that we should meet. / They suggested we meet up at Mustafa's.
	- **this** (adverb): "so" or "to this degree"
		- (O) this difficult, this cold, this far, this late
		- (X) this much difficult (this much, this much money는 맞음)
	- **unpack** vs. **unpacked**: unpack이 동사일 때는 열다가 되지만 unpacked라는 상태는 "풀지 않은" 상태. unpacked = unopened (opened가 아님) (여전히 와 닿지는 않음)
	- **present simple** vs. **present continuous**: (과거 시제도 마찬가지)
		- She is working: temporary
		- She works: long-term situation
		- <a
			href="https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/grammar/c1/advanced-present-simple-continuous"
			target="_blank">Present Simple & Present Continuous</a>
		- I felt unwell (단순 사실) vs. I was feeling unwell (상태의 흐름)
