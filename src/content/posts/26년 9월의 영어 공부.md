- 강사의 권고
	- 문법 책을 꾸준히 공부해라. 예문을 손으로 쓰는 것이 외우는데 도움이 많이 된다.
- 새로 배운 단어 / 표현
	- **cluttered**: 집이 정돈이 되지 않은 상태는 unorganised 보다 cluttered가 자연스러움
	- **dusky**: dark in colour
		- dusky red / dusky pink
	- **gait**: a particular way of walking
		- He walked with a slow stiff gait.
	- **get the feel of something** / **get the feel of how to do something**
		- get the feeling of how to handle a boat
		- It took a long time before I really got the feel of riding.
	- **what the future holds**: If you say that the future holds something, you mean that that thing will happen
		- Who can tell what the future holds?
	- **hold someone/something up**: to delay someone or something
		- The traffic held us up
		- We were held up by an accident
	- **hunk**:
		- a large, thick piece, especially of food: a hunk of bread / cheese / meat
		- a tall, strong, attractive man
	- **more of** + a [Noun] **than**: ~라기 보다는 ~에 가깝다.
		- He is more of a friend than a boss.
		- It's more of a hobby than a career for me.
		- Rather than X, it's more of Y.
	- **Not only but also** 를 전체 절에 적용 (Not only 절의 Inversion 주의)
		- Not only did it take hours, but it also drained my energy.
		- It didn't just take hours - it drained my energy too.
	- **sedentary** (/ˈsed.ən.tər.i/): involving little exercise or physical activity
		- My doctor says I should start playing sport because my lifestyle is too sedentary.
	- **stroke**(noun)에 쓰다듬다는 의미도 있음. An act of moving your hand, another part of the body, or an object gently over something or someone, ususally repeatedly and for pleasure
		- Don't be frightened, just give the horse a stroke.
	- 비양심적에 가까운 표현: shameless
	- **It took + person + amount of time + to do something**: journey 자체의 duration 보다 그 사람의 경험에 조금 더 focus
		- It took **me** three hours to finish the book.
		- It took **her** 20 minutes to get ready.
		- It took **us** an hour to get home.
	- quotation marks("") 사용 주의. 강조의 의미로 사용하지만, 영어에서 많은 경우 doubt나 irony로 읽힘
- #### 틀리게 / 부정확하게 알고 있었던 내용
	- 부사 위치 주의
		- became **completely** sunny
		- should **always** be
	- gerund vs. infinitive 주의
		- the **first time making** (O) / the first time to make (X)
		- **problem**, **difficulty**, **trouble** 모두 ing
			- problem with -ing / difficulty -ing / trouble -ing
		- **ability**, **chance**, **opportunity**와 같은 미래를 향하는 명사는 to + verb
			- ability to - / chance to - / opportunity to -
	- 요일과 마찬가지로 last week, last year 등 기간 앞에 next/this/last 등이 오면 전치사 생락
	- **appropriate**는 판단을 수반하는 다소 강한 표현이라.
		- it isn't appropriate 보다는 it doesn't feel appropriate로 완화시키거나
		- is isn't suitable for를 사용
	- **call**은 타동사. call ***to*** somebody라고 하지 않도록 주의. call somebody (O)
	- **consider**는 as를 쓰지 않음
		- consider A ~~**as**~~ B / call A ~~**as**~~ B
			- I condider myself lucky / Do you consider him a friend of yours?
		- 반면에, regard A **as** B / see A **as** B / view A **as** B / describe A **as** B
	- **cost**(verb)의 과거, 과거 완료는 모두 cost. costed(X)
	- **I don't know why, but I guess ..** 는 정확하지 않은 표현. guess를 한다는 건 확실하진 않지만 완전 모르지는 않은 상태라, **I'm not sure why,** 라고 하는 것이 정확함.
	- **fee**: countable noun임. 관사 필요.
		- a parking fee / an admission fee
	- **feedback**: 전치사는 **on**을 쓰고 **uncountable**임
		- feedback of (X) feedback from (X) / I had three feedbacks (X)
	- **get up** vs. **wake up**: wake up는 잠에서 깨는 것. 눈 뜨는 것. get up은 침대에서 나오는 것.
	- (class) **hold** vs. **run**: class organiser가 hold 하고 class는 run 또는 take place 함
		- they **hold** a class / the class **runs** every Tuesday
	- **home** (there, inside, outside 등도 마찬가지) 앞에 전치사 to를 쓰지 않도록 주의
		- the way back to home (X) the way back home (O)
	- **hope**는 object + to 부정사를 못 씀
		- I hope him to continue (X) / I want him to continue (O)
		- I hope he continues (O)
		- wish, expect, would like는 want와 같이 사용 가능
	- **an hour and a half** or **one and a half hours**: one and half hour (X)
	- **how** something **looks** / **what** something **looks like**
		- how something looks like (X)
	- **improve**가 자동사로도 쓰임. 꾸역꾸역 수동태로 쓰지 않도록 주의. 특히 현재 완료나 현재 진행일 때.
		- My English is being improved (X) / My English is improving (O)
		- My English has been being improved (X) / My English has been improving (O)
		- **change, grow, develop, increase** 다 마찬가지.
		- 외부 주체가 개선을 시키는 명확한 상황은 예외.
			- the system is being improved by the developers.
		- 다시 정리하면,
			- My English has improved. (O, 가장 자연스러움)
			- I've improved my English. (O, 내가 노력했음을 강조)
			- My English has been improved (by him). (드물게, 내가 아닌 다른 주체가 개선 시켜준 경우)
	- **nearby**는 부사 또는 형용사. 전치사처럼 쓸 때는 near를 써야 함.
		- is located **nearby** / a **nearby** park / is located **near** my office (O)
	- **pain**은 Uncountable. **-ache**는 Countable
		- shoulder pain / a headache
	- **reason**에는 **for**를 써야 됨. by 가 아님.
	- **tee-off**는 동사로 쓰이고 (we teeed off at 1:30) 시각을 지칭할 때는 그냥 tee time
	- **the whole** vs. **all**
		- the whole + singular: the whole book, the whole day, the whole time
		- all + plural: all five books, all the students
	- **wonder**:
		- I just wondered: 질문을 부드럽게 할 때
		- It made me wonder: 마음속에 궁금증이 떠 오를 대
- 입에 붙게 연습할 내용
	- ~한 김에
		- **Since I was already** out, I grabbed some groceries. (가장 편안한 표현)
		- I washed my car, and **while I was at it**, I cleaned the inside too.
		- **Taking advantage of** already being in XX, I stopped by the YY. (불필요하게 복잡..)
	- **One downside** is ...
	- **not for any other reason but simply for** ... 더 간단하고 Casual하게는 **for no other reason than**
	- **I'd done** something **before, but it was the first time I'd done** something else.
	- **ended up**:
		- We tried to leave early, but we **ended up** staying until midnight.
		- I only planned to buy one book, but I **ended up** buying five.
		- We tried to order as little as possible, but we **ended up** with an expensive bill.
	- **It's been** *period* **since I last** *did something*.
	- **to some extent**
		- To some extent, stress can be a useful motivator.
